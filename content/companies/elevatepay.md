ElevatePay, formerly Elevate, offers US-based dollar accounts to people working for clients abroad. It is backed by Y Combinator and raised $5M, according to Aaj.

It supports USD rather than a range of currencies, according to a user quoted in a competitor's comparison, so it may suit people paid only in dollars. Its fees and current Pakistan status need checking directly with the company.
