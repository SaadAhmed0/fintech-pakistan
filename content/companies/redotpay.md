RedotPay offers a crypto card, a USD virtual account and custodial balances. Its terms name a group of entities in Hong Kong, Argentina, Panama and the United States.

Its terms list USDT and USDC among supported assets, and describe the card as available in virtual or physical form. Reviews aimed at Pakistani users report that the card works there as a virtual card only, so check the app before you pay an issuance fee.
