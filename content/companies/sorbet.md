Sorbet builds cross-border payments on stablecoin rails. It describes itself as a financial technology platform rather than a bank, and says licensed bank partners handle fiat conversions.

Customers can open USD, EUR, GBP and AED account details, collect payments, and pay out to local bank accounts. In July 2026 Sorbet added payouts in Pakistani rupees, Saudi riyals and UAE dirhams, according to Fintech News Pakistan.

Sorbet is a partner of Fintech Pakistan.
