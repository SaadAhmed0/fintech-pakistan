ZAR raised $12.9M in a round led by Andreessen Horowitz, with $20M raised in total, according to Cointelegraph. Its founders, Sebastian Scholl and Brandon Timinsky, previously sold SadaPay to Papara.

Cointelegraph describes a model where people swap cash for stablecoins at local stores, phone kiosks and money agents by scanning a QR code, then hold them in a mobile wallet connected to a Visa card.
