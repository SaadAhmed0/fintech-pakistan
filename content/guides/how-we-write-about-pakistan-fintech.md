Every article here answers one question for the people building fintech in Pakistan, and every number links back to where it came from. This post explains how we get there.

## Primary sources first

We start with the people who publish the data: the State Bank of Pakistan, PVARA, SECP and company press releases. Reputable coverage comes second, and only for what the primary source doesn't say.

If we can't trace a figure to a source, we leave it out and say so.

## Short on purpose

Most of what you need from an article fits in a few minutes. The most important fact goes in the first two sentences, and section headings are written the way people search, like [how Raast works](/guides/raast-instant-payments-explained).

<p class="pull">If a number has no source, <em>it doesn't run.</em></p>

## Updated when the facts change

Pakistan's rules and numbers move quickly. When the facts in an article change, we correct it and show the date it was updated. The address stays the same, so anything you've shared keeps working.

## Why it matters for builders

If you're building on these rails, you should be able to check our work. Follow the links, read the source and tell us what we missed in the [community](/community).
