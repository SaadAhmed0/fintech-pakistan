Our mark is a pixel F with one block missing. Add the block and the F becomes a P. Fintech, completed by Pakistan.

That block is the job we've taken on. The people building financial services in Pakistan are spread across companies, cities and countries. Founders, operators, regulators and banks rarely end up in the same conversation, and fintechs arriving from abroad don't know where to start.

## What we're building

- **FP Community.** A free WhatsApp community with four channels: General, News & Regulation, Ask & Help, and Jobs & Opportunities. [How to join](/community).
- **Money Moves.** A weekly podcast with the people building how Pakistan pays, saves and sends. [About the show](/podcast).
- **FP Live.** Monthly meetups, closed-door roundtables and the Rails Summit in Lahore. [See events](/events).
- **FP Agency.** Launches, stories and stages for fintechs that want to reach this audience. [Partner with us](/partners).
- **Insights.** Short articles where every number links to its source.

## Alongside, not instead

Pakistan already has industry associations doing policy and advocacy work. We don't replace them. We're the room where everyone meets, and we'd like them in it.
