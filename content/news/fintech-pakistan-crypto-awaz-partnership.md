Fintech Pakistan and [Crypto Awaz](/partners/crypto-awaz) are exploring ways to work together to bring global fintech innovation closer to Pakistan.

Through this strategic partnership, the two platforms aim to connect international fintech companies with Pakistan's emerging digital finance ecosystem. The goal is to help them build local awareness, engage relevant communities, establish industry connections and navigate the local market.

## Who Crypto Awaz is

Crypto Awaz describes itself as the biggest crypto community in Pakistan, with members across Discord, X, Instagram and LinkedIn. Fintech Pakistan brings the wider fintech side: founders, operators, banks and regulators, through [our community](/community) and [FP Live events](/events).

## What the partnership covers

- **Fintech PR and communications.** Telling a company's story in a voice Pakistan's fintech and crypto audiences read.
- **Market-entry support.** Helping companies understand the local market before and after they launch.
- **Community engagement.** Putting companies in front of the communities that matter to them.
- **Industry insights.** Sharing what is happening in Pakistan's payments, banking and digital asset space.
- **Strategic introductions.** Connecting companies with the right people in the ecosystem.

## Why now

Pakistan's rules for digital finance are moving quickly. In 2026 the country set up a permanent virtual asset regulator and opened licensing for virtual asset service providers. [Read what PVARA's licensing regime sets out](/guides/pvara-virtual-asset-licensing). Companies entering the market need visibility and credibility as much as a licence.
