Fintech Pakistan started with two colleagues and one question.

Nauman Ali Murad and Saad Ahmed Siddiqui were working together at Sorbet, close to the stablecoin and neobank companies changing how money moves across borders. The more we saw of that world, the more one thing stood out.

Other markets had plenty of help. There were agencies and communities whose whole job was launching a fintech into a new country: telling its story, finding its first users and opening the right doors.

Pakistan had none of that. Crypto and fintech were taking off here, yet there wasn't a single place built to help a neobank launch in this market.

{% pull %}
If nobody was doing this for Pakistan, _why not us?_
{% /pull %}

So we decided to become that place. Fintech Pakistan is a home for the people building money here, and a launch partner for the companies coming to join them.

We're just getting started.
