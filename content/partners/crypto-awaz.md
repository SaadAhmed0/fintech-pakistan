## About Crypto Awaz

Crypto Awaz describes itself as the biggest crypto community in Pakistan. It brings Pakistani crypto users together across Discord, X, Instagram and LinkedIn.

## What the partnership covers

Fintech Pakistan and Crypto Awaz are working together to bring global fintech innovation closer to Pakistan. The partnership aims to connect international fintech companies with Pakistan's digital finance ecosystem, helping them:

- build local awareness
- engage relevant communities
- establish industry connections
- navigate the local market

The collaboration can span fintech PR and communications, market-entry support, community engagement, industry insights, and introductions to the right people in the ecosystem.

<p class="pull">The shared goal: help fintechs entering Pakistan <em>build visibility and credibility.</em></p>
