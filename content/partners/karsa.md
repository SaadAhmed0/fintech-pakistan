## About Karsa

Karsa offers multi-currency business banking. It was part of Y Combinator's Winter 2025 batch.

## What Karsa offers

- **One account, several currencies.** Balances in currencies including US dollars, euros, British pounds, UAE dirhams and the USDT stablecoin.
- **Global rails.** Receive, hold and send money over ACH, wire, SWIFT, SEPA and stablecoin transfers.
- **Built for businesses.** Accounts for companies that get paid by, and pay, customers and suppliers in other countries.

## Why it matters for Pakistan

Pakistani companies that sell to clients abroad, from software houses to exporters, need a reliable way to get paid in foreign currencies and move that money home. Multi-currency accounts are one of the building blocks for that.
