name: Stablecoins
description: News, guides and company profiles on stablecoins in Pakistan, from USDT and USDC accounts to the neobanks and cards built on digital dollars for local users.
---

Stablecoins are digital tokens designed to hold a steady value, usually one US dollar. This topic tracks how they reach people in Pakistan: the apps and cards that use them, how they are regulated, and what is confirmed versus only reported.
