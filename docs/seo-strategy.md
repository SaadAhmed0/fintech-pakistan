# Fintech Pakistan SEO and content strategy

Last updated: 5 November 2026. Owner: FP Admin.

## 1. How to read this document

**Goal:** become the first and most authoritative English result for stablecoin neobanks and fintechs entering or serving Pakistan, and turn that trust into inbound leads from companies wanting coverage or growth help.

**Evidence limits:** this plan was built without Ahrefs, Semrush or Search Console data, because none are connected yet.

- Demand and competition ratings (High, Medium, Low) are judgement calls based on reading live search results in November 2026. They are not measured search volumes.
- Replace them with real numbers after 60 to 90 days of Search Console data (section 8).
- Company availability below comes from sources read on 5 November 2026. It changes often and must be rechecked before publishing.

## 2. What the search results show

Reading results for the patterns in this plan showed four things:

1. **Pakistan-specific coverage is thin.** General "best stablecoin neobank" lists rarely mention Pakistan at all. A search for stablecoin neobanks available in Pakistan returned global lists with no Pakistan eligibility information.
2. **The results are dominated by parties with an interest.** Competitor blogs (Fasset, nsave), affiliate card sites (SpendNode) and Payoneer alternative pages fill most results. Independent coverage is rare. Some ProPakistani pieces carry a "Publishing Partner" byline, and we found no disclosure in the text we read.
3. **Sources disagree, and nobody reconciles them.** Whether a card ships physically, whether Wise can receive, and whether any exchange holds a full PVARA licence all have conflicting answers. Being the source that shows what is confirmed versus reported is our strongest position.
4. **Regulation moved fast and most explainers are low quality.** Many are law firm marketing pages or unofficial blogs, and some conflict on dates and licence status.

**Our edge:** dated, sourced, independent answers that separate company claims, third-party reports and regulator confirmation. The directory's "Needs verification" labels are the product, not a weakness.

## 6. Newsroom playbook

### Publishing within hours

1. **Confirm:** find the primary source (regulator, company announcement or filing). If you only have a report, say "reported".
2. **Write the first version in under an hour:** four to six short paragraphs. What happened, who it affects in Pakistan, what is confirmed, what is not yet known.
3. **Fill in the template:** `content/news/_template.md`, with the publish time in `date` (Pakistan time, such as `2026-11-05T14:30:00+05:00`).
4. **Check the company:** make sure it has a file in `content/companies/`. If not, add a stub with `draft: false`. It stays out of Google until verified, and mentions still link to it.
5. **Deploy:** the news sitemap regenerates on every deploy. It only lists the last two days of news, so publish before you promote.
6. **Share:** the WhatsApp community and social accounts, once they exist.

### Headline formula

`[Company] [does what] for [Pakistani audience]: [why it matters]`

- Name the company first, because people search for it.
- Stay within 60 characters where you can.
- State the fact. Never ask a question or tease.
- Good: "Sorbet adds PKR payouts for Pakistani freelancers"
- Weak: "Big news for freelancers in Pakistan"

### What to update later

| When | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Same day | Add company responses, correct errors, set `updated` |
| Within 48 hours | Add context, link the relevant guide, add fees and availability if confirmed |
| Within one week | Update the company profile and `lastVerified` |
| When the facts change | Update the story, set `updated`, keep the URL |

### Linking to the company profile

This is automatic. The first mention of a company name in the body links to its profile, and the story appears under "News about [company]" on that profile. To make sure it works, use the exact company name, or add spellings to `aliases` in its file.

### Partner and client news

Any story about a partner, client or paying company must set `label: Partner` or `label: Sponsored`. The disclosure then appears automatically. This is not optional, because it protects the whole site's trust.

## 8. Measurement plan

### Set up first (one-time)

- [ ] Add `www.fintechpakistan.com` to the Vercel project and redirect it to the main domain. HTTPS on `www` currently shows a certificate error.
- [ ] Verify the domain in Google Search Console and submit `sitemap.xml` and `news-sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Verify in Bing Webmaster Tools and import from Search Console.
- [ ] Add the site in Google Publisher Center. This helps but does not guarantee Google News inclusion.

### Monthly review

| Metric | Source | What to do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impressions and clicks by cluster | Search Console, filtered by URL path | Find which cluster is growing, and publish more there |
| CTR by page | Search Console | Rewrite titles on pages with high impressions and low CTR |
| Average position by query | Search Console | Update pages sitting in positions 5 to 15 first |
| Index coverage | Search Console, Pages report | Fix crawled but not indexed pages. Expect noindex on unverified profiles |
| New queries | Search Console | Add real questions to guides as FAQs |
| Core Web Vitals | Search Console, CrUX | Fix anything amber or red |
| Profiles verified | `content/companies` | Target: every profile rechecked within 60 days |
| Leads | Once capture is built | Inbound enquiries by source page |

### Cadence

- **Weekly:** check Search Console for indexing errors after new posts.
- **Monthly:** run this review and update the first 30 priorities.
- **After 60 to 90 days:** replace the estimates in section 5 with real data, and re-rank.

## 9. Risks and open questions

- **PVARA scope:** PVARA's FAQ says an activity may be in scope where it "targets, solicits, promotes to, or onboards persons in Pakistan", and advisory PVARA/ADV/001/2026 says announced pilots enabling services need prior authorization. Promoting an unauthorised provider to Pakistanis, especially as paid work, could create risk. Take legal advice before selling growth services.
- **No public register found:** PVARA's FAQ says it publishes a public register of licensees, but we could not find its URL. Until we do, `pvaraStatus` stays empty on every profile.
- **Karsa:** on 5 November 2026 gokarsa.com showed only a "Karsa | Export your wallet" page. Confirm its status before stating anything about its current product.
- **Authorship:** Google News and YMYL content both favour named people. FP Admin works for launch, and real bylines should follow.
- **Thin existing posts:** all eight posts are under 300 words.
- **Conflict of interest:** partner companies appear in the directory with a Partner label. Keep this consistent as client work grows.
